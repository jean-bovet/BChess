BChess is a free, open-source chess app for iPhone, iPad and Mac with its own chess engine. It plays entirely offline and collects no data.

PLAY
- All the rules of chess, including castling, en passant and promotion. Checkmate, stalemate, the fifty-move rule and threefold repetition end the game.
- Play against the computer or a friend, or watch the computer play itself.
- Five computer strengths, from Beginner to Maximum. Below Maximum the computer takes a moment before it replies, like a human player.
- Promote a pawn by picking the piece over the board.

UNDERSTAND
- The Engine button shows who stands better, the evaluation and the best line, including on your own turn.
- The full move list in standard notation: one tap away on iPhone, beside the board on iPad in landscape and on the Mac.
- Step back and forward through the game.

Open source: the full source code is on GitHub at https://github.com/jean-bovet/BChess
